KEY DESIGN ELEMENTS:

Tab Bar:
  • Located at top of window
  • Shows all available tabs
  • Active tab highlighted in blue
  • Inactive tabs in dark gray
  • Icons for quick recognition

Sections:
  • Collapsible headers with icons
  • Clear, descriptive titles
  • Organized content
  • Consistent spacing
  • Professional appearance

Colors:
  • Active Tab:     #4080FF (Blue)
  • Inactive Tab:   #1F1F28 (Dark Gray)
  • Background:     #0F0F12 (Very Dark)
  • Text:           #FFFFFF (White)
  • Accent:         #64B6FF (Light Blue)
  • Success:        #50FF96 (Green)
  • Warning:        #FFD400 (Yellow)
  • Error:          #FF5050 (Red)

Spacing:
  • Window Padding:  12px
  • Frame Padding:   8px, 6px
  • Item Spacing:    8px
  • Section Gap:     12px

Typography:
  • Headers:        Bold, larger font
  • Labels:         Regular, medium font
  • Values:         Regular, medium font
  • Descriptions:   Smaller, lighter color

Layout:
  • Responsive:     Adapts to window size
  • Organized:      Logical grouping
  • Consistent:     Uniform appearance
  • Professional:   Clean, modern design
